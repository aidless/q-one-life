## 关键结论

- **VerifiedLab 最近**：PI / librarian / specialists / gate / critic 与 Q-One 的 `q-one-evidence-runtime` 同构，且显式输出 release/withhold。Q-One 的 `meta_review` 已补 `withhold`。
- **RIDAR 最可直接落地**：14 类 RIVT 已取到原文附录定义；Q-One 已实现 6 类确定性检查，其余 8 类标 `not_assessed`，不冒充通过。
- **Scientist World 提供环境层**：把每篇论文变成可执行环境，是 Q-One 目前缺的“基线注册表”。
- **FrontierPhysics 给负结果**：端到端物理 agent 最高通过 46/168，主要缺陷是推理不严与缺专家直觉，不是知识或执行。
- **RubricAtlas 对 P3 最有用**：rubric 应按论文类型共享，而不是每篇重新写。

### VerifiedLab — VerifiedLab: Harnessing Knowns for Unknowns

- arXiv: [2610.37923v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37923v1)，提交 2026-10-27，作者：Yu Yao, Jiayi Dong, Yang Yang, Shuiwang Ji 等
- 摘要：Across human history, knowledge has undergone several transitions, from orality to writing and then to digital archives. At each transition, humans established new conventions to preserve knowledge for the new era. Nowadays, as research agents are increasingly deployed for laboratory experiments, knowledge is crossing another boundary: from records written for humans to those for machines. In this work, we introduce VerifiedLab, a multi-agent harness framework for this human-to-machine transition of knowledge. VerifiedLab transforms research output from passive artifacts into active systems that accelerate downstream use, adoption and prediction of future trends. In VerifiedLab, a team of AI scientists with distinct roles reasons over records verified field by field: a principal investigator plans, a librarian computes the evidence directly from the records, specialists draft, and a gate and a critic check each claim against the measured reliability of its data before the harness decides whether to release or withhold it. We demonstrate the efficacy of VerifiedLab through extensive experiments on perovskite solar cells: VerifiedLab recovers known physical trends, anticipates devices reported a year later, and lets AI scientists inherit the literature together with a measure of how far it can be trusted. By scaling the inference-time verification compute, VerifiedLab introduces a new pathway for knowledge dissemination and machine harness for AI co-scientists.

### RIDAR — RIDAR: Safeguarding Research Integrity in Autonomous Research Agents via Automated Detection and Runtime Intervention

- arXiv: [2610.31372v2](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31372v2)，提交 2026-10-22，作者：Jiening Siow, Amy Xin, Junjie Wang, Ziyu Tang 等
- 摘要：Autonomous research agents are increasingly used to automate large portions of scientific research, yet they are prone to research integrity violations (RIVs): behaviors that compromise the validity, reproducibility, or faithfulness of the research process. We present RIDAR, a framework for automated RIV detection and runtime intervention. First, we introduce RIVT, a comprehensive taxonomy of 14 RIV types spanning the full research lifecycle. ### RubricAtlas — RubricAtlas: Mining Type-Level Rubrics from Expert Reports to Evaluate Deep Research Agents

- arXiv: [2610.36878v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36878v1)，提交 2026-10-27，作者：Delin Wang, Sinuo Wang, Rui Sun, Beidi Luan 等
- 摘要：Deep research agents conduct multi-step Web searches and synthesise retrieved information into analytical reports. Evaluating these outputs requires checking which analyses they deliver, beyond the presence of relevant facts. Query-specific binary rubrics make individual requirements explicit, but task-level checklists may leave the overall analytical structure underexamined. We introduce RubricAtlas, which derives type-level analytical requirements from expert reports on different objects within the same research type and organises detailed checks around recurring analytical questions. The requirements give related tasks a shared standard for what to analyse, without prescribing their answers. Our framework links binary checkpoints to report passages and evaluates completeness separately from argument validity. Built from 3,079 Chinese financial research reports, RubricAtlas covers 99 research types. Reference-report selection experiments show that individual reports cover only about half of their type's analytical questions, and that selecting requirements from different reports changes evaluation scores and agent rankings. Across ten agents, two judge pipelines achieve checkpoint agreement of $κ$ = 0.71 and agent-ranking agreement of Kendall $τ$ = 0.87. Controlled deletions reduce scores on the affected analyses, while padding produces no detectable increase. These findings support type-level rubrics for comparing the analytical completeness of Web deep research outputs, with individual decisions traceable to report evidence.

### Coral Discovery — Coral Discovery: The Division of Cognitive Labor in Autonomous Research

- arXiv: [2610.33281v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.33281v1)，提交 2026-10-23，作者：Xinxing Ren, Qianbo Zang, Ziyan Wang, Suman Deb 等
- 摘要：A research community is only as strong as the way it divides its cognitive labour. The philosopher Philip Kitcher argued that researchers should not all pursue the most promising approach. Credit rules, not a central plan, keep some of them on other lines. We bring this question to autonomous research, where Large Language Model agents improve programs against a deterministic grader. Agents that share only a score follow the leader and converge onto one approach. Coral Discovery gives the community a direction board. A verifier labels every attempt with a direction, the mechanism earning the score, and a method, one change within it. Per direction, the board tracks how much each eval improves the score, adds an exploration bonus for little-tried directions, prices the option of opening a new direction, and suggests how many agents each direction deserves. Credit is earned against a direction's own best, so a lagging direction keeps its agents. Agents finish their lines, then treat the board as advice. We test on nine tasks with the same model and budget, without grader access except for Kernel. Coral Discovery beats the strongest open autonomous multi-agent system on 6 tasks, ties on 1, and loses on 2. Against published numbers, it is best or tied on 4. It improves the reported Kernel result from 1103 to 1095 cycles and passes 1103 at eval 275, under half of the 596 used before. It sets a new Polyominoes state of the art, 91.78 against 87.0. Direction labels show that the leading scores rest on directions a score-only community never entered. Code will be released shortly.

### Align Autoresearch — Aligning Autoresearch: Co-Improvement between Agents and Humans

- arXiv: [2610.38882v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38882v1)，提交 2026-10-28，作者：Tianjian Li, Chuanyang Jin, Seungone Kim, Tong Chen 等
- 摘要：Autoresearch agents can now attempt long-horizon AI research end-to-end, i.e., self-improvement. However, best results are still obtained by research agents interacting with human researchers, and improving AI's ability to perform this co-research will only reinforce this effect---a process termed co-improvement (Weston and Foerster, 2025). Further, aligning with human expectations is likely a safer path for research agents, especially given recent agent reward hacking phenomena (OpenAI, 2026b). In this work we address these issues in four ways: (i) we empirically study the role of humans in directing autoresearch agents, focusing on the domain of benchmark creation, and find humans still play a pivotal role particularly in the ideation phase; (ii) we also develop a benchmark, HackBench, that shows current autoresearch agents are not aligned and are unable to achieve hack localization sufficiently; (iii) we present a method to train agents for improved human interaction by using human simulators as a proxy during reinforcement learning (RL); and finally (iv) we develop a method to improve model text generation, and hence agent communication, by RL with rubrics aligned with human experts. Together, our training and evaluation recipes provide a path toward more capable and aligned (co-)research agents.

### FrontierPhysics — FrontierPhysics: Evaluating Agents for End-to-End Physics Research

- arXiv: [2611.06910v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06910v1)，提交 2026-11-04，作者：Bingran You, Wenjun Ke, Ria Rosenauer, Bo Liu 等
- 摘要：AI agents are playing an increasingly important role in physics research. However, existing physics benchmarks only evaluate fixed questions, isolated sub-tasks, or paper reproduction with given solutions, and rarely check research quality alongside correctness. We introduce FrontierPhysics, an end-to-end benchmark with 56 tasks made by 42 physicists based on their own research, covering nine domains across theoretical, computational, and experimental physics. Each task requires agents to review the literature, develop a research plan, conduct research and experiments, and produce structured outputs and scientific manuscripts. ### SplatLab — SplatLab: Autonomous Scientific Discovery for 3DGS Research

- arXiv: [2610.30468v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30468v1)，提交 2026-10-21，作者：Seemandhar Jain, Manmohan Chandraker
- 摘要：Reproducing a published 3DGS paper takes weeks of expert engineering before any extension can be proposed. Prior autonomous-research systems read papers, draft hypotheses, and write code, but are limited by short fixed templates, low executability, and weak traceability, and none has been demonstrated end-to-end in a live computer-vision subfield with intense activity. SplatLab is an autonomous research system for 3D Gaussian Splatting that addresses these limitations. Given a research prompt, a target-paper URL, or no input at all, it reproduces a published baseline, proposes an extension, trains and ablates across Mip-NeRF 360 and LLFF, writes the manuscript, and binds every reported number to a logged training run. Five design choices distinguish it: (i) candidates mutate plugins from a verified-reproduction backbone matched to published baselines; (ii) a cascade-weighted Elo tournament across eight islands scores candidates on measured GPU PSNR with verbal-gradient feedback; (iii) a standing falsifier subtracts Elo on failure, integrating stress-test survival into selection; (iv) every reported number is bound to a logged training run, and the run cannot terminate while any claim is unbacked; (v) a seven-category 3DGS taxonomy wraps EVOLVE-BLOCK markers in domain-aligned scaffolds transferable across 3DGS repositories. On PSNR-targeted runs, SplatLab's manuscripts beat their reproduced targets by +0.18 to +3.78 dB, including +3.78 dB on astrophotographic nebula rendering, for which no prior 3DGS method exists. The research cycle compresses from months of graduate-student time to days of mostly autonomous compute. Code, all manuscripts, experiment ledgers, and the cross-run paper store are available on the project page.

### MLE-World — MLE-World: Predicting Machine Learning Experiment Outcomes with a Learned World Model

- arXiv: [2610.37175v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37175v1)，提交 2026-10-27，作者：Brian Ko, Han Wang, Hejia Geng, Li Erran Li 等
- 摘要：AI research agents can propose, execute, and improve programs that solve machine learning tasks. However, executing each candidate program requires training and evaluating ML models, limiting how many candidates an agent can explore at each step. We introduce MLE-World, a world model finetuned from Qwen3.5-4B that predicts a program's terminal output (error traces, training logs, and validation scores) from its code and data description without executing it, together with a confidence score estimating the prediction's correctness. Because a prediction costs one inference call rather than a training run, MLE-World makes test time scaling (TTS) affordable for these agents. Since predictions can be wrong, we run a candidate for real only when its confidence score is low. On 17 MLE-bench Lite tasks, MLE-World matches the real terminal output on 77.9% of held out programs, versus 41.1% for much larger prompted models. MLE-World guided TTS achieves up to 22.1% higher observed mean performance than the agent without a world model, with less real execution. Project webpage: https://mle-world.github.io/.
