# Pipeline

1. Freeze canonical and variant sources; record byte hashes and the frozen
   input snapshot.
2. Recompute manifests and reject stale/missing entries without modifying the
   upstream tree. Follow every declared BibTeX file; resolve citation keys and
   separately record bounded external identity checks.
3. Recompute grid aggregates, T30/T50 seed-level statistics, T50 per-seed ECE
   from archived confidence/correctness pairs, real-pilot CV and coupling, and
   detect corrupt/duplicate arrays. Do not infer per-round ECE from the pair data.
4. Bind every headline number to a typed carrier; keep absent or historical
   references blocked.
5. Run claim-ledger verification and retain the generated report.
6. Register a blocked Research Factory overlay. Compile only a labelled internal
   revision until evidence closes; package, upload, author metadata and any
   external action remain user gates.
