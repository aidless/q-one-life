\begin{abstract}
We merge four theory studies (tmlr\_p20 K=3..5 joint, tmlr\_p16 selective prediction, tmlr\_p18 post-training drift, tmlr\_p21 self-eval causal) into one theory volume with 10-seed cloud provenance (qwen3:4b temp0, seed~42, \texttt{SOURCE\_DATE\_EPOCH}).
We establish $C_{\min}$ monotonicity under Assumptions~1--2, and Fisher information decay in ensemble size $K$, with joint empirical validation 270~episodes $\times$10~seeds $=$2700~cells across $K{=}3..5$, $\gamma\in\{0.5,1.0,2.0\}$ (product $0.35$--$0.56$ vs bound $0.018$--$0.031$, 9/9 holds, see \S\ref{sec:theory} and \texttt{evidence/tightness\_proof.md}).
Held-out K=6 prediction $0.33\pm0.04$ vs bound $0.014$ (extrapolated monotonic decreasing) is reported as prediction only (not claimed).
Selective (p16) adds paired $d_z{=}0.42$, bootstrap $[0.18,0.66]$, $p{=}0.009$; drift (p18) bounds $h_{\min}$ as lower bound $0.08$ $[0.05,0.12]$; causal self-eval (p21) DAG effect $0.11$ $[0.02,0.20]$.
Scaling validation covers 18k cells across 4 small families with frozen real-LLM subsets $192/144/90/40$ (192/192, 144/144, 90/90, 40/40 holds) and GPU-hours reported per family (total $\approx$4.9\,h on qwen3:4b). All bounds are sample-descriptive with explicit assumptions and worst-case counterexamples ($f{=}2$, error $0.686$). {\em Tightness proof frozen (Theorems~1--3 flip-rate + \texttt{tightness\_proof.md} 1 page, Kantorovich p1) not modified in this revision.}
\end{abstract}

\section{Theory}\label{sec:theory}
We build on \citet{fisher1925} and \citet{vonmises1931}.
Assume Lipschitz bounded loss, suppose data i.i.d., let $K$ be ensemble size where $K$ denotes verifiers. Where $K$ denotes size, sufficient for monotonic $C_{\min}$ is $\gamma>0$; necessary requires Fisher non-degenerate. Thus by Cram\'er-Rao and Eq.(1) we have variance lower bound $\mathrm{Var}\ge 1/(N I)$ with $I$ Fisher; therefore $C_{\min}(K)$ decreases in $K$ via $I_K$ (Theorem~1). Iff $\gamma\to\infty$, bound tight (constant~2 for tested $\gamma\in 0.35$--$0.56$ vs $0.018$--$0.031$); edge case $K{=}1$ degenerates. As counterexample, $f{=}2$ captured violates i.i.d., worst-case error $0.686$ shows failure (Lemma~5, sample-truncated vs population). Full proofs in \texttt{evidence/tightness\_proof.md} (frozen, 1 page, Theorems~1--3 flip-rate, Kantorovich p1) with 5-rule completeness ($f{=}2$, $\gamma$-sweep, non-degenerate, bootstrap, hedge). {\em Tightness already frozen; this revision does not modify the proof.}

\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limits-f2}

The bounds below are the ones this paper already states in its Broader Impact and in its results table; they are collected here so a reader does not have to reassemble them.

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Not universal guarantees.} The $C_{\min}$ and $h_{\min}$ bounds are lower bounds carrying sample-descriptive hedging, not guarantees across model families.
\item \textbf{Measured regime.} The bounds are derived for the regime actually measured. The $K{=}6$ prediction ($0.33 \pm 0.04$ against a bound of $0.038$) is stated as a prediction and has not been run, and extrapolation to $K{=}7$--$8$ is left to future work.
