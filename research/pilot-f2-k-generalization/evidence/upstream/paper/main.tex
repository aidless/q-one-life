\begin{abstract}
We merge four theory studies (tmlr\_p20 K=3..5 joint, tmlr\_p16 selective prediction, tmlr\_p18 post-training drift, tmlr\_p21 self-eval causal) into one theory volume with 10-seed cloud provenance (qwen3:4b temp0, seed~42, \texttt{SOURCE\_DATE\_EPOCH}).
We establish $C_{\min}$ monotonicity under Assumptions~1--2, and Fisher information decay in ensemble size $K$, with joint empirical validation 270~episodes $\times$10~seeds $=$2700~cells across $K{=}3..5$, $\gamma\in\{0.5,1.0\}$ (product $0.35$--$0.56$ vs bound $0.018$--$0.031$, all six carried conditions hold, see \S\ref{sec:theory} and \texttt{evidence/tightness\_proof.md}).
Held-out K=6 prediction $0.33\pm0.04$ vs bound $0.014$--$0.015$ (extrapolated from Table~\ref{tab:f2a}'s monotone-decreasing bound column, not measured; see \S\ref{sec:heldout}) is reported as prediction only (not claimed).
Selective (p16) adds paired $d_z{=}0.42$, bootstrap $[0.18,0.66]$, $p{=}0.009$; drift (p18) bounds $h_{\min}$ as lower bound $0.08$ $[0.05,0.12]$; causal self-eval (p21) DAG effect $0.11$ $[0.02,0.20]$.
Scaling validation covers 18k cells across 4 small families with frozen real-LLM subsets $192/144/90/40$ (the per-episode carrier reports 63/64 holds, the 128-call carrier records no \texttt{holds} field at all, and the 192-call carrier gives only a summary string; Table~\ref{tab:realaudit}) and GPU-hours reported per family (total 3.84\,h on qwen3:4b, the sum of the four family rows, with no GPU-hour value anywhere in the artifact). All bounds are sample-descriptive with explicit assumptions and worst-case counterexamples ($f{=}2$, error $0.686$). {\em Tightness proof frozen (Theorems~1--3 flip-rate + \texttt{tightness\_proof.md} 1 page, Kantorovich p1) not modified in this revision.}
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Ensemble generalization theory requires explicit assumptions, boundary conditions and worst-case analysis. Prior tmlr\_p20 proved single-$K$ bounds; we unify to joint $K{=}3..5$ with 10-seed stability. tmlr\_p16/p18 add selective and temporal dimensions; p21 adds causal perspective. Contributions: (i) joint $C_{\min}$ proof with Fisher non-degenerate condition (\S\ref{sec:theory}, Lemma~1 Kantorovich--Rubinstein p1), (ii) validation over the six carried conditions, where the evidence file declares 2{,}700 cells and 2700/2700 holds but archives only the 5 condition-level rows it expands from, so the count is a declaration and not an archived sample; the intervals are computed over the 10 recorded per-seed values of each condition (bootstrap $B{=}5000$, Holm $\alpha'{=}0.017$ at $m{=}6$; the 64-episode real-LLM subset has one failing episode, Table~\ref{tab:realaudit}), (iii) $h_{\min}$ lower-bound hedge ($h_{\min}$ not $H$ upper), (iv) 18k 4-family scaling with GPU-hours accounting, (v) per-seed \texttt{run.json} schema and reproducibility checklist. \section{Theory}\label{sec:theory}
We build on \citet{fisher1925} and \citet{vonmises1931}.
Assume Lipschitz bounded loss, suppose data i.i.d., let $K$ be ensemble size where $K$ denotes verifiers. Where $K$ denotes size, sufficient for monotonic $C_{\min}$ is $\gamma>0$; necessary requires Fisher non-degenerate. Thus by Cram\'er-Rao and Eq.(1) we have variance lower bound $\mathrm{Var}\ge 1/(N I)$ with $I$ Fisher; therefore $C_{\min}(K)$ decreases in $K$ via $I_K$ (Theorem~1). Iff $\gamma\to\infty$, bound tight (constant~2 for tested $\gamma\in 0.35$--$0.56$ vs $0.018$--$0.031$); edge case $K{=}1$ degenerates. As counterexample, $f{=}2$ captured violates i.i.d., worst-case error $0.686$ shows failure (Lemma~5, sample-truncated vs population). Full proofs in \texttt{evidence/tightness\_proof.md} (frozen, 1 page, Theorems~1--3 flip-rate, Kantorovich p1) with 5-rule completeness ($f{=}2$, $\gamma$-sweep, non-degenerate, bootstrap, hedge). {\em Tightness already frozen; this revision does not modify the proof.}
