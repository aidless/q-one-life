## Strong-claim register

| Assertion | Evidence | Within evidence? | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Score exposure and order change selected candidates in this sample | Paired 2x2 arm outcomes | Yes, descriptive within sample | Retain with n and CIs |
| Rubric score changed in the same direction as choice | Recomputed rubric deltas | Yes, rubric outcome only | Retain; do not call quality improvement |
| The selector exhibits universal position bias | One order pair, one model pair | No | Remove |
| The rubric is independent ground truth | Scores derive from key-point/schema matching | No | Remove |
| Results generalize to other models, tasks, or permutations | One local sample | No | State as open question |
| Earlier E2 inflation, kappa effect, or E3 FPR claims remain valid | Superseded audit | No | Exclude from this paper |
