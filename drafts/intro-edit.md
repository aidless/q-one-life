
\paragraph{What the rule certifies.} The rule below answers one question: does the leader's score exceed the score of the process that produced the gold? It does not estimate a ranking error rate, and it does not certify that two systems are distinguishable. A readable verdict is therefore a sufficient condition for refusal under the specific construction measured here, but clearing producer baseline is \emph{not} a necessary condition for every resolvable top ordering. This distinction follows from the synthetic counterexample bound to this release (see §5), which shows that with three perfect producers setting floor=1.0, systems at 0.9 and 0.5 remain deterministically ordered yet the gate refuses. A readable verdict is therefore a sufficient condition for refusing when the construction fails, and not a guarantee that the ordering below it is valid.
